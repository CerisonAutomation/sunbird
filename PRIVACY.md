# Privacy Policy — Sunbird

**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

## Introduction

Sunbird ("we", "our", "the game") is a free-to-play browser game. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you play our game.

## Data We Collect

### Game Data (Local Storage)
- Game progress (score, coins, skins unlocked)
- Settings (sound, music, language preferences)
- Leaderboard scores (personal bests)
- Achievement progress

**This data stays on your device.** We do not collect or transmit this data.

### Platform Data
When playing on portals (Poki, CrazyGames, etc.), the platform may collect:
- Play time and session data
- Ad impressions (anonymous)
- Device type and browser info

This data is controlled by the platform, not by us.

### Payments (Standalone Only)
If you purchase Gold or VIP on our standalone version:
- Payment is processed by Stripe
- We do not see or store your credit card information
- Stripe's privacy policy applies: https://stripe.com/privacy

## Data Usage

We use collected data to:
- Save your game progress
- Displayleaderboards
- Improve game performance
- Serve ads (through platform partners)

## Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Sunbird is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our game does not require account creation or personal data.

## Your Rights

- **Access:** View your local game data in browser settings
- **Delete:** Clear browser data to remove all game progress
- **Opt-out:** Disable ads in game settings (where available)
